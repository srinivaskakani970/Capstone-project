[HIGH] In May 2026, sales jumped to INR 138,738.93 across 77 orders - a month-on-month increase of +122.19%. [HIGH] This is the single largest-magnitude MoM swing observed across all 9 active regions in either the April-to-May or May-to-June transition. [HIGH]

**Key Insight:** Guntur's April-to-May sales increase of +122.19% is an outlier against the dataset's typical regional volatility. [HIGH] The order count rose from 51 to 77 (+50.98%), indicating that the sales increase is driven by both higher order volume and higher average order value (April AOV: INR 1, 224.36; May AOV: INR 1,801.80). [HIGH] This was followed by a -28.11% decline from May to June (INR 138,738.93 to INR 99,745.18, 62 orders), suggesting the May spike may be transient rather than a sustained growth trend. [MEDIUM]

[LOW] Guntur was flagged in both transitions: +122.19% in April-to-May and -28.11% in May-to-June, both exceeding the 8% operational-alert threshold. [HIGH] No other region exhibits a comparable single-transition swing (the next largest is Visakhapatnam at -62.46% April-to-May, which has a known dataset-level multiplier of 0.45 applied in the generator). [HIGH]

[MEDIUM] If the spike is concentrated in one or two categories, investigate whether it reflects a one-time bulk order or a repeatable demand pattern.
